# Personal Site

The personal presentation site of Jaroslav Lufinka, built to win developer interviews on the Czech market. Its entire job is to convince a technical Reader that its author ships real software.

## Language

**Reader**:
The person the site is written for: an engineer or engineering manager evaluating Jaroslav for a role. Every content decision is judged against what a Reader would conclude.
_Avoid_: user, visitor, recruiter, audience

**Project**:
A substantial body of work presented on the site. BikeCheck is the first; lufihome is expected to follow. Small exercises and learning repositories are not Projects.
_Avoid_: portfolio item, work sample, app

**Selected Work**:
The home page section that lists Projects. Holds one Project at launch and is built to hold several.
_Avoid_: portfolio, projects section, showcase

**Case Study**:
The long-form article about a single Project, living at its own URL so it can be linked directly from a job application.
_Avoid_: project page, blog post, article, write-up

**Feature Tour**:
The part of a Case Study that establishes a Project's scope by covering its features briefly. Proves the Project is complete rather than a demo.
_Avoid_: overview, feature list, summary

**Deep Dive**:
The part of a Case Study that opens up one hard problem in full: the constraint, the options weighed, the choice made, and what it cost. Proves the author reasons rather than assembles. A Case Study has two or three.
_Avoid_: chapter, section, highlight, breakdown

**Walkthrough**:
The silent screen recording that shows a Project actually running. Stands in for a live demo where none is possible.
_Avoid_: demo, video, trailer, preview

**Proof Link**:
An outbound link that lets a Reader verify a Project exists independently of the site's own claims — a store listing or a source repository.
_Avoid_: CTA, external link, button
