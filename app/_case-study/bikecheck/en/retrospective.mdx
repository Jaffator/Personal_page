The decision that paid off was putting wear on the server as a derived value. It
was slower to build than storing a number and updating it, and it is the reason
there has never been a bug where a component's wear disagreed with the rides that
caused it.

The decision to revisit is the offline model. It is correct for the case it was
built for and awkward for the case that turned up later, which is a rider logging
several rides at once after a weekend away.

What I would do differently is write the seed data first. Most of the time lost to
debugging early on was time spent constructing, by hand, the state a test needed.
