The application is complete rather than a demonstration. A rider can:

- Register a bicycle and record the components fitted to it
- Log rides, by distance and by time, against a bicycle
- See accumulated wear per component, derived from those rides
- Record a service — a replacement, a clean, an adjustment — and reset what that resets
- Read the full service history of a bicycle, or of one component across bicycles
- Use the application in Czech or in English, with the language following the device

None of this is behind a feature flag or a stub. The list is short because the
application does one thing, which is the point.
