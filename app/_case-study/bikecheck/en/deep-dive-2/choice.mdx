The fitment period. A component is fitted to a bicycle on a date and removed on a
date, and that record is the only thing written down. Total wear, wear since the
last service, and wear on one bicycle are all the same query with different
bounds.

<Figure caption="A wheelset's fitments across two bicycles. Wear for any question is the rides falling inside the relevant bars.">

Moving a wheelset is then closing one fitment and opening another — an event that
actually happened, rather than a correction to a stored number.
