BikeCheck tracks the service history and component wear of a bicycle. A drivetrain
wears out on a schedule its owner cannot see, and the cost of missing that schedule
is a chain that takes the cassette with it. The application makes the invisible
part legible: what has been fitted, how far it has run, and what is due.

It is built as an Android application on **Capacitor**, with a **NestJS** API and a
**PostgreSQL** database behind it. What follows is how it is put together, and the
three decisions that were genuinely hard.
